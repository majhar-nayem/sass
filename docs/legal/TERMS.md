to it — called "you" below. They cover the website builder, the hosting of the website
it produces, and anything else we provide with it.

We have tried to write them in plain English. Where a term sounds one-sided, it usually
is not allowed to be: you are a small business, and standard-form contracts with small
businesses are subject to the unfair contract terms rules in the Australian Consumer
Law. If something here reads as unfair to you, tell us — we would rather fix the term
than argue about it.

## 1. What we provide

We provide a service that builds a website for your business from what you tell us
about it, lets you edit that website, and publishes it on the internet at a subdomain we
provide or a domain you own.

We do not provide legal, tax, marketing or business advice, and nothing the AI writes is
any of those things.

## 2. What you are responsible for

The content of your website is yours. You are responsible for it being true.

That matters more than it sounds. The AI writes a first draft from what you tell it, and
it is quite capable of writing a confident sentence about your business that is not
accurate. **Read your site before you publish it.** Claims about your business —
qualifications, licences, years of experience, awards, prices, guarantees, what you can
do and how fast — are representations you are making to your customers, and the
Australian Consumer Law holds you to them, not us.

We build guardrails against the obvious failures: the system will not invent
testimonials, will not fabricate credentials or awards, and will not write a countdown
to a deadline that does not exist. Those guardrails reduce the risk. They do not
transfer it.

You are also responsible for:

- having the right to use any image, logo or text you upload
- your own licence and registration obligations for the trade you are in
- the personal information your website collects from your customers, and for having a
  privacy policy that describes it (we generate one, but it is yours)
- keeping your account credentials to yourself

## 4. Fees, GST and price changes

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and **include GST**.

Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually in advance and renew automatically until
you cancel. We will email you before each annual renewal.

If we change the price, we will give you at least **30 days' notice by email** before it
takes effect, and you may cancel before then without penalty. A price change never
applies to a period you have already paid for. If you do not want the new price, cancel
— we would rather that than have you feel trapped.

Where we offer a fixed price for an initial term, that price is fixed for that term.

## 5. Cancelling, and getting your website back

You can cancel at any time, from your account, without ringing anyone and without
explaining yourself.

When you cancel, your subscription runs to the end of the period you have paid for and
then stops. We do not refund the unused part of a period you chose to end early, except
where the Australian Consumer Law requires it — and if we have got something badly
wrong, it does.

**Your content is yours and you can take it with you.** On request within 90 days of
cancelling, we will give you an export of your site's text and images, and we will help
you point your own domain somewhere else. We do not hold your domain, your content or
your customer enquiries hostage to keep you subscribed.

After 90 days we delete your site and its content. Say so before then if you want it
kept.

## 6. Your customers' personal information

Your website collects personal information from your customers — names, phone numbers,
email addresses, whatever they type into an enquiry form. **That information is yours,
not ours.** We hold it on your behalf and we only use it to run the service for you.

We do not sell it. We do not use it for our own marketing. We do not use your customers'
personal information to train AI models.

If there is a data breach affecting it, we will tell you promptly and work with you on
what the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

offer a service credit scheme at this price — if that is something your business needs,
we are probably not the right fit and we would rather tell you now.

We will give you notice of planned maintenance where we reasonably can.
