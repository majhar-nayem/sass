> **Draft for legal review. Not in force. Not legal advice.**

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](./TERMS.md). It says what you may not
publish or do with a website we host. It is short because most of it is obvious.

## Don't publish

- Anything unlawful, or anything that promotes or helps someone do something unlawful
- Content that infringes someone else's copyright, trade mark or other rights —
  including images you found online and do not have a licence for
- Sexual content involving children, in any form. This is the one thing we report to
  the police rather than merely remove.
- Content that incites violence or hatred against people because of who they are
- Harassment of an identifiable person, or content published to intimidate someone
- Malware, phishing pages, or anything designed to deceive a visitor into handing over
  credentials or money

## Don't mislead people about your business

This is the one that catches honest operators out, so it is worth being specific. On a
website we host, do not:

- claim qualifications, licences, registrations, insurance or accreditations you do not
  hold
- publish testimonials or reviews that are not from real customers who really said that
- state a price that is not the real total price a customer will pay, including GST
- advertise a discount against a "was" price that was never the actual selling price
- run a countdown or a "limited time" offer with a deadline that is not real
- claim to be the cheapest, the best, the only or the oldest unless you can substantiate it

The Australian Consumer Law prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct, and it applies to
you as the business making the claim. Our system refuses to generate several of these
and warns about others, but you can override a warning and you can type anything you
like into an editor. If you do, it is your representation.

## Don't misuse the service

- No sending unsolicited bulk email through the service. If you collect email
  addresses, the Spam Act 2003 requires consent, accurate identification of the sender
  and a working unsubscribe.
- No using the site to collect personal information you have no reason to collect
- No attempting to access another customer's data, or to probe, scan or interfere with
  the service
- No reselling or sublicensing the service without our agreement
- No automated or bulk use of the AI features beyond what a person could plausibly do

## Regulated trades and claims

Some industries have their own advertising rules on top of the Australian Consumer Law
— health services, financial services, legal services, alcohol, therapeutic goods,
gambling. If you are in one of those, those rules are yours to comply with and they
override anything the AI suggests. We do not check them for you.

## What happens if you breach this

Usually we will email you, tell you exactly what the problem is, and give you a
reasonable chance to fix it. Most breaches are someone not realising, and a phone call
sorts them out.

For content that is seriously harmful or unlawful, we will take the site down first and
tell you straight after. For child sexual abuse material we will also report it.

We keep a record of what we removed and why, and you can ask for it.

## Reporting a problem

If a site we host is breaching this policy, email **abuse@awning.au** with the address
of the page and what the problem is. See `TAKEDOWN-RUNBOOK.md` for what happens next.
